NuGet restore in CI: Frequent Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

Most NuGet restore in CI problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.
