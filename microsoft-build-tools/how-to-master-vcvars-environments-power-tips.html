Why vcvars environments Matters in 2024

Setting Up vcvars environments the Right Way

Before changing anything, take five minutes to write down what bothers you about the current setup. Is it build speed? Debugging? Deployment? Targeted fixes to vcvars environments stick better than wholesale rewrites driven by curiosity.

Every experienced developer has lost an afternoon to at least one of the issues below. The pattern behind all of them: a default left unexamined, or an assumption imported from a different ecosystem where the rules are subtly different.
