FAQ

Is F# a good first language?

For learners who like structured thinking, yes — its emphasis on modeling data precisely and avoiding null teaches excellent habits. Most developers, however, arrive at F# after some C# or another mainstream language.

Can I use interfaces in F# in an existing project, or only in new ones?

Both. Retrofitting interfaces in F# into an existing project is common — introduce it incrementally, module by module, rather than in one sweeping change. New projects should simply start with it enabled from day one.
