pipelines and composition: Frequent Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

Most pipelines and composition problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Copy-pasting configuration from an old project without understanding what each block does — audit every imported setting once
  • Mixing tool versions across the team; pin versions in the repo and let the toolchain enforce them
  • Skipping the clean/rebuild check before assuming a bug is real — stale artifacts explain a remarkable share of “impossible” errors

Conclusion

The takeaway is simple: make pipelines and composition a deliberate choice rather than an accident of history. Document what you adopt, automate what you can, and prune what stops earning its place. That discipline compounds across every project you touch.
