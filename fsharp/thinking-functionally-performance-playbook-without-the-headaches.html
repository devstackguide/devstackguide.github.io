The Case for thinking functionally in 2023

Setting Up thinking functionally the Right Way

Version pinning deserves a special mention. Lock the toolchain version in the repository so every developer and every CI agent sees identical behavior, and upgrade on your schedule — not when a random warning forces your hand.

  • Enable the strictness or validation options early — retrofitting them later is far more painful
  • Schedule a quarterly review to prune settings that no longer earn their place
  • Wire the basic commands into your editor so everyday tasks are one keystroke away

Common Mistakes with thinking functionally (and Fixes)

Most thinking functionally problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Mixing tool versions across the team; pin versions in the repo and let the toolchain enforce them
  • Treating local and CI environments as different worlds; the same commands should run in both
  • Hand-editing generated files; find the source of truth and change it there instead

Conclusion

The takeaway is simple: make thinking functionally a deliberate choice rather than an accident of history. Document what you adopt, automate what you can, and prune what stops earning its place. That discipline compounds across every project you touch.
