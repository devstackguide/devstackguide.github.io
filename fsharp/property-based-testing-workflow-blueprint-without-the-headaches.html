property based testing: Frequent Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

Most property based testing problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Hand-editing generated files; find the source of truth and change it there instead
  • Skipping the clean/rebuild check before assuming a bug is real — stale artifacts explain a remarkable share of “impossible” errors
  • Ignoring warnings until the output is a wall of noise — fix, suppress with a comment, or configure thresholds deliberately

Conclusion

property based testing rewards a small, consistent investment far more than a heroic one-off effort. Start with the checklist above, keep the setup boring and documented, and revisit it once a quarter. The rest of your workflow gets quietly easier from that.
