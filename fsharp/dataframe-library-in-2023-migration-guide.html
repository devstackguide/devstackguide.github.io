FAQ

Can F# and C# live in the same solution?

Absolutely — they compile to the same IL and interoperate in both directions, with small conventions to respect (F# prefers interfaces and options; C# sees them as ordinary .NET types).

What is the biggest mistake beginners make with DataFrame library?

Skipping the fundamentals and jumping straight to advanced configuration. Understand the default behavior first; most “problems” with DataFrame library are actually undocumented expectations colliding with sensible defaults.
