How long does it take to get comfortable with regular expression source generators?

Most developers feel productive within a few days of focused practice and solid within a month of real project work. The concepts click quickly; the edge cases are what experience fills in over time.

When should I use a record instead of a class?

Records fit value-like data: DTOs, messages, and immutable snapshots where equality by value and non-destructive mutation (with expressions) matter. Behavioral types with identity and lifecycle remain regular classes.
