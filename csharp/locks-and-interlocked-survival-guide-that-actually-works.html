Setting Up locks and Interlocked the Right Way

Start small and grow the setup as the project demands it. The most common failure mode is importing a giant configuration from the internet, half of which nobody on the team understands. Every setting below exists for a reason — adopt it deliberately.

  • Keep all locks and Interlocked-related files in source control, including local overrides you choose to ignore deliberately
  • Enable the strictness or validation options early — retrofitting them later is far more painful
  • Set up a minimal CI job that runs the same commands developers run locally

Common Mistakes with locks and Interlocked (and Fixes)

Most locks and Interlocked problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.
