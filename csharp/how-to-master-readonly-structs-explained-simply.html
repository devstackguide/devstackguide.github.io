FAQ

When should I use a record instead of a class?

Records fit value-like data: DTOs, messages, and immutable snapshots where equality by value and non-destructive mutation (with expressions) matter. Behavioral types with identity and lifecycle remain regular classes.

Is readonly structs still relevant in 2024?

Yes — more than ever. The ecosystem around readonly structs keeps growing, and the skills transfer directly between projects. What changes year to year is mostly the tooling surface, not the underlying concepts, so time spent.
