FAQ

What is the biggest mistake beginners make with collection expressions?

Skipping the fundamentals and jumping straight to advanced configuration. Understand the default behavior first; most “problems” with collection expressions are actually undocumented expectations colliding with sensible defaults.

When should I use a record instead of a class?

Records fit value-like data: DTOs, messages, and immutable snapshots where equality by value and non-destructive mutation (with expressions) matter. Behavioral types with identity and lifecycle remain regular classes.
