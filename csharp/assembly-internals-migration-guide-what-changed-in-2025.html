FAQ

Where can I find reliable documentation for assembly internals?

Start with the official documentation, then curated community guides. Be skeptical of tutorials older than two years — the 2025 landscape looks noticeably different from even the 2022 advice still ranking in search results.

When should I use a record instead of a class?

Records fit value-like data: DTOs, messages, and immutable snapshots where equality by value and non-destructive mutation (with expressions) matter. Behavioral types with identity and lifecycle remain regular classes.
