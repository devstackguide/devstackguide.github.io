Setting Up staying on 4.8 safely the Right Way

Common Mistakes with staying on 4.8 safely (and Fixes)

Most staying on 4.8 safely problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Mixing tool versions across the team; pin versions in the repo and let the toolchain enforce them
