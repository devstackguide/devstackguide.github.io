ESU-style considerations: Frequent Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

Most ESU-style considerations problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Copy-pasting configuration from an old project without understanding what each block does — audit every imported setting once
  • Ignoring warnings until the output is a wall of noise — fix, suppress with a comment, or configure thresholds deliberately
  • Treating local and CI environments as different worlds; the same commands should run in both

Conclusion

Tooling should serve the code, not the other way around. If your ESU-style considerations setup makes simple tasks harder, simplify it ruthlessly. The best configuration is the one nobody on the team has to think about.
