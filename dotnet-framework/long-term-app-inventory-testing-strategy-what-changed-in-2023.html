Getting Started with long-term app inventory

Before changing anything, take five minutes to write down what bothers you about the current setup. Is it build speed? Debugging? Deployment? Targeted fixes to long-term app inventory stick better than wholesale rewrites driven by curiosity.

long-term app inventory: Frequent Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

Most long-term app inventory problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Skipping the clean/rebuild check before assuming a bug is real — stale artifacts explain a remarkable share of “impossible” errors
  • Copy-pasting configuration from an old project without understanding what each block does — audit every imported setting once
  • Hand-editing generated files; find the source of truth and change it there instead

Conclusion

Tooling should serve the code, not the other way around. If your long-term app inventory setup makes simple tasks harder, simplify it ruthlessly. The best configuration is the one nobody on the team has to think about.
