Common Mistakes with cost of doing nothing (and Fixes)

Most cost of doing nothing problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Skipping the clean/rebuild check before assuming a bug is real — stale artifacts explain a remarkable share of “impossible” errors
  • Hand-editing generated files; find the source of truth and change it there instead
  • Copy-pasting configuration from an old project without understanding what each block does — audit every imported setting once

Conclusion

The takeaway is simple: make cost of doing nothing a deliberate choice rather than an accident of history. Document what you adopt, automate what you can, and prune what stops earning its place. That discipline compounds across every project you touch.
