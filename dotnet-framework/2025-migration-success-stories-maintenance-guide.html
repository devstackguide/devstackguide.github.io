Common Mistakes with migration success stories (and Fixes)

Most migration success stories problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Mixing tool versions across the team; pin versions in the repo and let the toolchain enforce them
  • Treating local and CI environments as different worlds; the same commands should run in both
  • Skipping the clean/rebuild check before assuming a bug is real — stale artifacts explain a remarkable share of “impossible” errors

Conclusion

The takeaway is simple: make migration success stories a deliberate choice rather than an accident of history. Document what you adopt, automate what you can, and prune what stops earning its place. That discipline compounds across every project you touch.
