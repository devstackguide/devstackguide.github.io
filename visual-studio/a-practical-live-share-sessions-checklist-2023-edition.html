Live Share sessions Setup, Step by Step

The workflow itself is straightforward once the pieces are in place. Most of the value comes from the first hour of setup, after which the tooling quietly does its job in the background while you focus on actual product work.

  • Schedule a quarterly review to prune settings that no longer earn their place
  • Keep all Live Share sessions-related files in source control, including local overrides you choose to ignore deliberately

Common Mistakes with Live Share sessions (and Fixes)

Every experienced developer has lost an afternoon to at least one of the issues below. The pattern behind all of them: a default left unexamined, or an assumption imported from a different ecosystem where the rules are subtly different.

  • Skipping the clean/rebuild check before assuming a bug is real — stale artifacts explain a remarkable share of “impossible” errors
  • Ignoring warnings until the output is a wall of noise — fix, suppress with a comment, or configure thresholds deliberately
  • Mixing tool versions across the team; pin versions in the repo and let the toolchain enforce them

Conclusion

Tooling should serve the code, not the other way around. If your Live Share sessions setup makes simple tasks harder, simplify it ruthlessly. The best configuration is the one nobody on the team has to think about.

FAQ

Can I use Live Share sessions in an existing project, or only in new ones?

Both. Retrofitting Live Share sessions into an existing project is common — introduce it incrementally, module by module, rather than in one sweeping change. New projects should simply start with it enabled from day one.

Where can I find reliable documentation for Live Share sessions?

Start with the official documentation, then curated community guides. Be skeptical of tutorials older than two years — the 2023 landscape looks noticeably different from even the 2020 advice still ranking in search results.
