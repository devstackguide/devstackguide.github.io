editor themes and fonts: Frequent Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

Most editor themes and fonts problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Skipping the clean/rebuild check before assuming a bug is real — stale artifacts explain a remarkable share of “impossible” errors

  • Ignoring warnings until the output is a wall of noise — fix, suppress with a comment, or configure thresholds deliberately
