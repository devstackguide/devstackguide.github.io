Hot Reload workflows: Frequent Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

Most Hot Reload workflows problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Hand-editing generated files; find the source of truth and change it there instead

  • Mixing tool versions across the team; pin versions in the repo and let the toolchain enforce them
