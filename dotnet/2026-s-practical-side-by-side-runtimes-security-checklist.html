Getting Started with side-by-side runtimes

Version pinning deserves a special mention. Lock the toolchain version in the repository so every developer and every CI agent sees identical behavior, and upgrade on your schedule — not when a random warning forces your hand.

  • Enable the strictness or validation options early — retrofitting them later is far more painful
  • Keep all side-by-side runtimes-related files in source control, including local overrides you choose to ignore deliberately
  • Add a short README section so a new teammate can get a working environment in under 30 minutes

side-by-side runtimes: Frequent Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

Most side-by-side runtimes problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Hand-editing generated files; find the source of truth and change it there instead
  • Treating local and CI environments as different worlds; the same commands should run in both
  • Ignoring warnings until the output is a wall of noise — fix, suppress with a comment, or configure thresholds deliberately

Conclusion

None of the individual steps here are difficult — the value comes from doing them consistently across the team. Pick two improvements from this guide, apply them this week, and schedule the rest. Momentum beats perfection with side-by-side runtimes.
