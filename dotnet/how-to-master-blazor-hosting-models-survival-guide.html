FAQ

What is the difference between the .NET SDK and the runtime?

The SDK includes the compiler, build tools, and templates used to develop apps; the runtime is what executes a compiled app. Build machines need the SDK, servers often only need the runtime.

How long does it take to get comfortable with Blazor hosting models?

Most developers feel productive within a few days of focused practice and solid within a month of real project work. The concepts click quickly; the edge cases are what experience fills in over time.
