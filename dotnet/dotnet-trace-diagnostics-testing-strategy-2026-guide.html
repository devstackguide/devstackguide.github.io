Common Mistakes with dotnet-trace diagnostics (and Fixes)

Most dotnet-trace diagnostics problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.
