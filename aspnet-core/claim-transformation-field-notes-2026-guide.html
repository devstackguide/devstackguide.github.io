claim transformation Setup, Step by Step

Start small and grow the setup as the project demands it. The most common failure mode is importing a giant configuration from the internet, half of which nobody on the team understands. Every setting below exists for a reason — adopt it deliberately.

  • Schedule a quarterly review to prune settings that no longer earn their place

  • Set up a minimal CI job that runs the same commands developers run locally

Most claim transformation problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Ignoring warnings until the output is a wall of noise — fix, suppress with a comment, or configure thresholds deliberately

FAQ

Minimal APIs or controllers — which should I choose?

Minimal APIs shine for small focused services and microservices; controllers remain comfortable for large apps with lots of shared conventions, filters, and attribute-based behavior. Both are first-class and fully supported.

How do I keep my claim transformation setup up to date?

Subscribe to the official release notes, upgrade on a schedule (quarterly works well for most teams), and keep a changelog of your own configuration decisions so upgrades stay boring and reversible.
