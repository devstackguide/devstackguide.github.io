The Case for content items and globs in 2023

The reason content items and globs keeps coming up in 2023 is simple: projects grow, teams grow, and the tooling has to keep up. Getting this part right early prevents the kind of slow decay that turns a clean codebase into a maze of workarounds within a year.

Common Mistakes with content items and globs (and Fixes)

Most content items and globs problems are not exotic — they are the same handful of mistakes repeated across teams. Catching these early is the cheapest quality improvement available, and none of the fixes require deep expertise.

  • Skipping the clean/rebuild check before assuming a bug is real — stale artifacts explain a remarkable share of “impossible” errors
  • Hand-editing generated files; find the source of truth and change it there instead
  • Ignoring warnings until the output is a wall of noise — fix, suppress with a comment, or configure thresholds deliberately

Conclusion

Tooling should serve the code, not the other way around. If your content items and globs setup makes simple tasks harder, simplify it ruthlessly. The best configuration is the one nobody on the team has to think about.
