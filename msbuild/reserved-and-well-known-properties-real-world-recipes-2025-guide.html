Where can I find reliable documentation for reserved and well-known properties?

Start with the official documentation, then curated community guides. Be skeptical of tutorials older than two years — the 2025 landscape looks noticeably different from even the 2022 advice still ranking in search results.

What is the difference between a property and an item?

Properties are single name-value strings used for configuration; items are typed lists (Compile, PackageReference, Content) that carry per-entry metadata and accumulate through Include/Remove/Update.
