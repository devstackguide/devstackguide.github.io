FAQ

How do I keep my item definition groups setup up to date?

Subscribe to the official release notes, upgrade on a schedule (quarterly works well for most teams), and keep a changelog of your own configuration decisions so upgrades stay boring and reversible.

What is the difference between a property and an item?

Properties are single name-value strings used for configuration; items are typed lists (Compile, PackageReference, Content) that carry per-entry metadata and accumulate through Include/Remove/Update.
